## Post-publication correction (same session, second commit)

Product Radar #3 (Victron BlueSolar MPPT 75/15) originally read that a per-panel
75/15 "caps the voltage a failed controller can hand your BMS at well under the
85V analog-front-end limit." Two faults, same root: it benchmarked against the
**85V AFE absolute maximum** when the Background establishes **60V** as the
operative working floor (because BMS output FETs are commonly 60V parts), and it
let the word "per-panel" carry the entire safety argument. The 75/15's nameplate
accepts **75V** of PV — which is two 200W panels in series, 74.88V at STC and
**86.46V at Collins' −30°F design temperature**, i.e. precisely his danger
example. A reader buying a 75/15 and series-stringing two panels lands in the
failure case the Lead describes. Rewritten to attribute the cap to the wiring
practice rather than the controller, to benchmark against 60V, and to state the
75V ceiling explicitly.

Community Pulse was softened in the same pass: the r/vandwellers diagram gives a
parts list but not a topology, so the original "nowhere near the voltages that
make a controller failure interesting" overclaimed. Series wiring of those two
panels is ~49V at STC — under the 60V floor, but not "nowhere near" it.

LESSON FOR FUTURE RUNS: when a Lead establishes a safety threshold, every later
section that names a product must be re-read against *that* threshold, not
against the headline number. The Background's 60V floor and the Lead's 85V AFE
ceiling are different numbers for different purposes and are easy to conflate.
