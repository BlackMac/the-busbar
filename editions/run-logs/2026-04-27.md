## REVIEW FLAGS

1. **Lead — Battle Born technical note interpretation.** The Lead summarises Battle Born's published claims and the independent investigators' responses without taking sides. Specific values quoted from Battle Born ("less than 200 microohms", "under two watts", "20 mV", "approximately 700 times", "0.4 %") are all directly from the company's published technical note (linked) — they are what Battle Born claims, not independently verified. Worth flagging because the framing of the story matters for owner safety perception.
2. **Multi RS v1.29 (March 26) and VictronConnect v6.32 (March 31) are 27–32 days old by edition date.** CLAUDE.md guidance allows up to 14 days; both are stretched beyond that. Justification: there were no fresher Victron firmware items not already covered in the previous edition, and the v6.30 regressions in particular have been actively affecting users for the full intervening period. If a reader notices the date stretch, the rationale is editorially defensible.
3. **JK BMS V19.31B firmware caution.** Sourcing leans on Andy Schroeder's video and the DIYSolarForum thread. JK does not publish a formal English-language changelog for this build. The "RS485 inverter communication issues" claim should be read as community/forum-reported, not vendor-confirmed.
4. **Casely Power Pods chemistry note.** The CPSC recall page describes the cells as lithium-ion and the units are USB / MagSafe wireless power banks. We described them as "different chemistry to ours" — accurate for our LFP-leaning audience. Confirm the safety message lands as intended (these are NOT LiFePO4 packs failing; they are lithium-ion consumer charger packs).
5. **China VAT rebate background.** Forecasts cited are from PV-Tech, ESS-News and VATupdate. The 10–14 % "consensus" figure is The Busbar's own synthesis — there is genuine disagreement in the market, with some sources quoting 30–40 % combined effect. The background reflects the bounded view; flagged for awareness.
6. **Battle Born forum links in Community Pulse.** The Airstream / iRV2 / Escape Trailer thread URLs were reported by web search, not click-verified. They may need a quick follow-up if any are 404 or if the threads have been moderated/closed.
7. **DIYSolarForum LG Chem ESS BMS thread** — referenced "BMS in LG CHEM ESS modules" as a 9 April thread, but linked to the BMS sub-forum index because the specific thread URL was not in the search results. Confirm and replace with a direct thread URL if possible before publication.
