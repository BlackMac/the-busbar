## REVIEW FLAGS
1. **Legal exposure — Lead.** The lead reports a defendant's account of active litigation (Dragonfly Energy v. Prowse). Every claim is attributed to Prowse, the piece states that Dragonfly's complaint alleges the opposite, that nothing is proven in court, and that no CPSC or NHTSA campaign exists on these packs. Dragonfly was not contacted for comment. **Requires human sign-off before publication.**
2. **Safety claim repeated.** Prowse's position that none of the affected packs are safe to use and should be disposed of is reported as his advice, explicitly not as a finding or a recall. Verify the framing reads as attribution, not endorsement.
3. **Forum tally, not data.** The "more than a hundred posts" failure count is Prowse's count of a DIYSolarForum thread. Labelled in-text as a forum tally rather than an audited dataset; we did not independently count the thread.
4. **Datasheet timing claim.** The "changed one day before the suit was filed" detail is Prowse's claim. We reported the underlying archived-datasheet discrepancy in edition 2026-06-29. Dragonfly has not publicly responded to the timing allegation; stated as such.
5. **Product Radar brand cap — breach found and FIXED before publication.** First draft had LiTime at 2 of 4 Product Radar items (50%) against the 40% cap, which AGENT_BRIEF §4 states with "no exceptions." The A1 and A2 entries were merged into a single "Argoseeker A1 & A2" card. Product Radar is now 3 items — LiTime 1, Gobel Power 1, Dakota Lithium 1 — at 33% each, all under cap, and still within the 3–6 spec. Both SKUs' specs and the 42A-continuous sizing note are preserved in the merged entry. `published-topics.md` intentionally retains two separate radar lines (A1, A2) for granular future dedup even though the rendered edition shows one card.
6. **Unverifiable model name.** Victron's own case study lists an "Orion XS 1400" DC-DC charger, which does not match any model in the current Orion XS line-up. Flagged in-text as unconfirmed rather than silently corrected or repeated as fact.
7. **No list pricing for Argoseeker.** LiTime is publishing launch pricing only behind promo codes. Product Radar entries say "Not published" rather than quoting a promo price that will expire. Revisit when a stable list price appears.
8. **Catch-up items, not window stretches.** The Dakota Lithium close (31 July) and Dragonfly Q2 (6 Aug) both fall inside the *previous* edition's 29 July–8 August coverage window and were missed there. Included here as catch-up. The Lead itself (14 Aug), LiTime (10 Aug), Gobel VRM (15 Aug) and both recalls are squarely in the current window.

Justified by genuine new developments (amended complaint, $4M acquisition, Q2 results) rather than a re-run, but the concentration is worth a human eye against brief §12 failure mode 2.
