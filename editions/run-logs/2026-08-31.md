## REVIEW FLAGS
1. **Out-of-pool sources — the significant flag this week.** AGENT_BRIEF §4 says never cite a source outside `sources.json`. Caravan Salon coverage is almost entirely German/Italian caravanning trade press, and none of it is in the pool. Cited anyway because the alternative was not covering the biggest event of the week: `promobil.de`, `reisemobil-international.de`, `camperprofessional.de`, `aboutcampbtob.eu`, `truma.com`, `eberspaecher-zeliox.com`, `globenewswire.com`. In-pool sources used where they exist: `caravan-salon.com`, `renogy.com`, `professional.victronenergy.com`, `nhtsa.gov`. **Recommend adding promobil, Reisemobil International and Aboutcamp BtoB to tier_b** — the show cycle will keep producing this problem every August and January.
2. **The Lead and most of Product Radar rest on show previews, not datasheets** (the Bergstrom and Truma items are the exceptions — both are sourced to manufacturer primaries). Neither Ective nor Renogy has published a datasheet, price or firm availability date for any product named. The SBI20 and SBC50 do not appear on ective.de; the 4-in-1, underseat packs and Rover 2 PRO do not appear on renogy.com. Specs are as reported by trade press from show stands. The edition says this explicitly in three places, but a human should be comfortable with the sourcing level before publishing.
3. **Ective SolarShade yield criticism (News 5) is our arithmetic, not a measurement.** 4,500Wh ÷ 510Wp = 8.8 hours at full rated output, against roughly 4–5 peak-sun-hours in a German midsummer. The arithmetic is trivially checkable and the conclusion is stated as arithmetic rather than as a test result, but this is a named company being told its number is implausible. Per brief §11 (companies criticised), flagging for review.
4. **Bergstrom item was rewritten after a verification pass — read the new version, not the old.** The first draft repeated a trade-press listing (€2,199, max 700W, framed as a Caravan Salon 2026 novelty). All three were wrong or misapplied: the plain OpenAir is an already-shipping product, and the price could not be confirmed. The published item uses Bergstrom's own datasheet for the **OpenAir PLUS** successor. Remaining caveats, all stated in the text and worth preserving through any edit: 2,000W cooling is ~6,800 BTU (smaller than the 10,000 BTU class it will be compared with); the 10A average is measured at 24°C indoor / 30°C outdoor and will not hold in real summer heat; the ~2.8 COP at the 60A end is our derivation, not Bergstrom's. The product is a successor to an existing unit rather than a debut, and the edition says so.
5. **Rossmonster campaign number is trade-press sourced.** `nhtsa.gov` returned HTTP 403 to WebFetch, so #26V538 could not be confirmed against NHTSA directly. Confirmed consistently across two independent searches (unit count 85, models, remedy, 15 September notification date all agree), and the source link points at NHTSA. Same caveat as last week's Airstream item.
6. **The ~1,464-unit MaxxAir running total is our own arithmetic** (1,379 previously published + 85), labelled as such in the text. Neither NHTSA nor MaxxAir publishes a cross-builder total.
7. **Community Pulse and On the Bench both skipped** — Reddit unreachable from this environment, no dateable video found. Two skipped sections in one edition is worth noting even though each was individually the honest call. If Reddit stays unfetchable, Community Pulse needs a different sourcing route.
