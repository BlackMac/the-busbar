Victron Venus OS 3.50 fixes MultiPlus-II AC dropout under float — 12,000 units affected
Venus OS 3.50 addresses a confirmed shutdown bug in MultiPlus-II 48/3000–48/5000 units (firmware HQ2518–HQ2611) where the inverter drops AC output after entering float — no warning, no alarm. If you have a MultiPlus-II 48V unit manufactured between November 2025 and February 2026, update now.
